About me

I am Scientific Director at the International Institute of Administrative Sciences (IIAS) and a Research Fellow at KU Leuven. My teaching cover statistics, quantitative methods and digital governance, through KU Leuven programmes and FLAMES.

My research examines how governance arrangements shape institutional performance and public outcomes, with applications in education, digital government and agroecological transitions. I combine quantitative methods with institutional analysis to understand how policies and institutions operate across different contexts.

I am currently developing the IIAS Governance Index, using composite indicators and frontier estimation to assess governance and identify country-specific pathways for improvement. My work on sustainability includes research on agroecological transitions in Peru and quantitative contributions to an Oxfam project on farming under climate pressures. I also work on online survey quality and AI applications in research.
