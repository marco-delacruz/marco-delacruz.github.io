Research

My research spans governance and educational performance, agroecological transitions, survey methodology and AI-assisted research. The four areas below outline my current questions, methods and related publications.

Research focus Publications Book Working papers Other contributions

Research focus

Governance, performance & education

How governance arrangements shape public-sector performance and education outcomes: intergovernmental coordination, performance regimes and school-system efficiency, using administrative data and international assessments (e.g., PISA, ECE). Methods: non-parametric frontier estimation (DEA, FDH, conditional and Benefit-of-the-Doubt estimators), composite indicators and regularised SEM. Current projects: the IIAS Governance Index and non-cognitive skills and mathematics achievement across Europe.

Related education paper PISA preprint

Sustainability & rural development

Institutional change in agroecological transitions: how formal arrangements and local practice intertwine when smallholders adopt new production and commercialisation models, building on the Ecological Economics ‘intertwining approach’. Qualitative fieldwork in Peru combined with comparative quantitative evidence from the SAMA project and an Oxfam study of family farming under climate pressure in Junín and Huancavelica.

Related agroecology paper Oxfam report

Survey methodology

Response quality in high-frequency online panels: non-parametric Benefit-of-the-Doubt scores to flag low-quality respondents, automated data-quality monitoring within the Total Survey Error framework, and topic modelling (LDA) of open-ended answers. Developed on the European Central Bank Consumer Expectations Survey and Eurobarometer data; presented at NTTS 2025 and AAPOR 2025.

NTTS contribution AAPOR poster

AI & research methods

Generative AI as a research instrument: computational literature reviews compared with traditional ones, LLM-assisted classification of qualitative data, and the division of labour between computation, interpretation and authorship. Taught as invited training at the European Parliamentary Research Service (EPRS) and Cedefop.
