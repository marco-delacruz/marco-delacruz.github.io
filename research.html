Marco De la Cruz

Research

My research connects governance and institutional performance with sustainability, education and digital government. It combines methodological development with applied research in Europe and Latin America.

Research focusPublicationsWorking papersProjectsReports

Research focus

Governance, performance & education

I study how governance arrangements shape public sector performance and education outcomes. My work combines institutional analysis, non-parametric efficiency estimation and composite indicators, building on my doctoral research on education systems in Peru. Current projects include the IIAS Governance Index and research on non-cognitive skills and mathematics achievement using PISA 2022.

Related education paper

Sustainability & rural development

I examine institutional change and agroecological transitions in Peru, including relationships between formal arrangements and local practices. This work includes my research in Ecological Economics, an advisory role on the social dynamics of the SAMA project, and quantitative work for an Oxfam project in Junín and Huancavelica addressing farming under climate pressures.

Related agroecology paper

Survey methodology

I develop non-parametric approaches for identifying low-quality responses in high-frequency online surveys. My applied work includes automated data-quality monitoring, survey error assessment and analysis of open-ended responses in international survey programmes, including work with European Central Bank researchers.

Related conference contribution

AI & research methods

I study how generative AI can support research workflows, particularly systematic literature reviews. My current work examines the relationship between computational tasks, interpretation and academic authorship, with further applications in quantitative social research.
